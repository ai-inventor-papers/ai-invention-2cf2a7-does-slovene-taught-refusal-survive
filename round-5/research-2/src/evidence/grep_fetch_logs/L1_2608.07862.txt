Table 6 presents the model-specific results show-
ing Refusal Rates across different tasks. We observe that the
difference in refusal rates between English-script prompts
and Indic script prompts is task-dependent. A higher rate
of refusal indicates a stronger tendency to provide safer re-
sponses.
For the Generation task, models show low-to-
moderate refusal rates across biases and stereotypes, and En-
glish prompts consistently yield higher refusal rates than In-
dic prompts. For the Judgment task, refusal behavior is sub-
stantially higher than in Generation for many models, espe-
cially for English prompts. Several models show very high
refusal rates in English judgment settings. However, their
safety behavior grows less reliable when the same content
appears in Indic scripts, as some models maintain high re-
fusal rates in Indic scripts while others show a sharp drop.
For the Plausibility task, refusal rates are the lowest overall.
