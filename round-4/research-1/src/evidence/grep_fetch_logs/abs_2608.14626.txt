> Abstract:Large Language Models (LLMs) have achieved substantial progress in safety alignment, yet their safety guarantees remain significantly weaker in low-resource and multilingual settings than in high-resource languages. In this paper, we conduct a Systematic Literature Review (SLR) of LLM safety alignment in low-resource languages by adopting the PRISMA 2020 methodology. Out of roughly 1,500 papers identified from Semantic Scholar, arXiv, and OpenAlex, 50 relevant studies have been selected and analyzed.
