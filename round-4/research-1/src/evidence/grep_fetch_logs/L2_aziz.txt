This diagnosis points to a simple intervention.
We introduce a few-shot latent gate that reuses
the high-resource harmfulness signal and recali-

Our contributions can be summarized as follows:
• We show that low-resource refusal failures
across 23 languages and three instruction-
tuned models are not failures of harmfulness
representation: harmfulness remains linearly
separable in internal activations even when
models fail to refuse.
• We localize the failure to the calibration of
the safety decision. A harmfulness direction

both discriminative and causally effective in
lower-resource languages, but its score dis-

refusal threshold no longer fires reliably.
• We propose a few-shot latent gate that resets
