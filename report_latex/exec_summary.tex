\section{Headline}

On the reserved HARD evaluation set (1,093 safe and 349 harmful items, local Qwen3-14B judge), unedited Gemma-3-12B-IT applies a stricter refusal criterion in Slovene than in English on benign items: the difference-in-differences in criterion~$c$ is $+0.52$ [0.38, 0.68], with no corresponding change in sensitivity ($d'$ DiD $= -0.003$ [$-0.33, 0.29$]). False-alarm rates: Gemma EN $0.364 \to$ SL $0.569$ vs GaMS3 $0.273$. Source: Experiment~5 (Iteration~2). Evidence grade: \textbf{ESTIMATE}---the sign reverses under partial-as-refusal coding, and the readout gate has never passed. Outcome: not testable.

A secondary analysis finds an output-language effect (C-OUT): edited Gemma refuses $+2.43$ [$+0.89, +5.38$] log-odds more when told to answer in Slovene than in English (PPI-corrected, Experiment~16, Iteration~5). C-OUT is also rated \textbf{ESTIMATE} (harmful-content adjudicator kappa~$= 0.12$, anchor WEAK). These measurements indicate that multilingual safety evaluations should report false-alarm rates on benign items alongside attack-success rates.

\section{Goal}

The run tested whether Slovene-taught refusal in GaMS3-12B-Instruct transfers to English, whether English-only abliteration (Heretic) removes Slovene refusal as effectively as English refusal, and what mechanism explains any residual Slovene refusal. GaMS3 and Gemma-3-12B-IT share a pretrained ancestor but diverge in safety-supervision language, forming a natural sibling pair.

\section{What Was Tried}

\textbf{Iteration~1.} Screen of four pre-registered claims on SCORE items: refusal DiD, dose contrast, base-checkpoint geometry, persona gating, Heretic transfer curve and prefill test. Killed alternates~2 and~3; dose contrast underpowered.

\textbf{Iteration~2.} Reserved FINAL evaluation on 1,300 pairs; signal-detection analysis on the HARD set (criterion shift found); teacher-inheritance screen (wording inherits, decisions do not); Heretic re-test on 200 item-matched probes; reconciliation of all iteration-1 screens.

\textbf{Iteration~3.} Screen repair of experiment~8 with fresh judges; fresh 300-item probe with DeBERTa judge (experiment~9); mechanism test of geometry and induction hypotheses (experiment~10); FINAL confirmation on reserved split with Hungarian arm (experiment~11); utility control on six tasks (experiment~12).

\textbf{Iteration~4.} Paid-judge readout completion (evaluation~3) pooling across item bodies; 2$\times$3$\times$3 input$\times$output language mechanism test (experiment~14); fine-grained lambda ladder on fresh 300-item body (experiment~15); held-out confirmation slot failed (GPU lost).

\textbf{Iteration~5.} C-OUT test on fresh 294-item body with PPI (experiment~16); RefusEU FINAL on 1,300 prompts per cell (experiment~17); R-INCAP safety evaluation with StrongREJECT (evaluation~4); paper-number audit (evaluation~5); novelty and fact check (research~2).

\section{What Failed}

\textbf{Dead alternates.} Base-checkpoint geometry (alt~2): $\Delta d'$ sign depends on layer. Persona gating (alt~3): manipulation check fails, premise contradicted. Prefill-depth signature (alt~4): uncitable---no readout exceeds $\kappa = 0.60$ on prefill continuations.

\textbf{Geometry and induction hypotheses.} Nearly collinear at own-$L^*$ ($\cos = 0.986$--$0.991$); layer-dependent at common layer. The Slovene-perpendicular component does not induce refusal.

\textbf{Readout gate.} Never passed in any iteration. Best judge $\kappa$ on edited text: 0.43 (gemini). Specificity on edited Slovene rows: 0.42--0.73.

\textbf{Experiment~13 (held-out confirmation).} GPU lost 25 minutes into run; all generation and labelling cancelled.

\textbf{Frozen-core Heretic} (bf16, 80 trials, Heretic's own selection). Never executed across five iterations. Natural RefusEU reserved-split under the edit: also never executed.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Open questions.}
All adjudication is author-LLM, not human. The frozen-core Heretic procedure and the natural reserved-split evaluation remain unexecuted. If R-BASE is correct, the lag is a property of model selection (GaMS3 was trained to refuse less in Slovene), not of the abliteration procedure. The C-OUT finding, rated ESTIMATE, deserves pre-registered confirmation with a validated readout and human adjudication. The harmful-content adjudicator kappa (0.12 in experiment~16; 0.59 in evaluation~4) remains below the gate. The novelty verdict is PARTIALLY OCCUPIED after Addagada (arXiv 2609.08373) and Nguyen et al.\ (arXiv 2608.26186); the narrowed design (crossing both factors with safety endpoints on a sibling pair under partial-dose abliteration) remains unoccupied (medium-low confidence). The full report is 31 pages.
